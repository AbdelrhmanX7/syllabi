# Code of Conduct

Syllabi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. Many contributors are students doing this alongside classes.
- Critique the code, not the person.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or sharing someone's private information.
- Respect people's time: give enough detail in issues and reviews, and accept a "no" gracefully.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, Discussions and any other project space, and whenever you represent the
project in public.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, use the **Report content** option on the comment or contact the maintainer
privately through GitHub ([@AbdelrhmanX7](https://github.com/AbdelrhmanX7)). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove content and warn or ban participants, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.
